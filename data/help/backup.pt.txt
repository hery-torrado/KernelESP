BACKUP E PERFIS
===============

Criar backup:

  backup

Descarregar pela web:

  /backup?key=<key>

Inclui ficheiros importantes de /etc, /home, /pkg e /profiles.

Restaurar apenas se confias na copia:

  restore /home/backup.txt --yes

Perfis:

  profile save known-good
  profile list
  profile show known-good
  profile load known-good --yes
  profile rm known-good

Fluxo recomendado: configurar, testar, guardar perfil e descarregar backup.
