PROFESSIONAL OPERATIONS
=======================

Before changing a live board:

  backup
  health
  free
  df
  wifi status
  jobs

Before a firmware update:

  tools/verify.sh
  tools/upload.sh <serial-port>

After serial upload, tools/upload.sh automatically sends:

  wifi sdkreset --yes

After updating LittleFS assets:

  tools/upload-assets.sh http://<esp-ip> <key>
  tools/smoke-http.sh http://<esp-ip> <key>

Read-only diagnostics:

  diag
  diag wifi
  tools/diagnostic-bundle.sh http://<esp-ip> <key>

Stable-change policy:

  - bug fixes
  - stability
  - security
  - documentation
  - ergonomics
  - memory reduction

Avoid adding firmware features unless they remove risk.
