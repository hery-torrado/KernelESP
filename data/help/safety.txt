SAFETY
======

KernelESP switches GPIO pins. It must not power mains loads directly.

For 110/230 V, pumps, heaters, motors, locks or valves:

  - use certified relay/contactor modules
  - isolate low voltage and mains wiring
  - use fuses and proper enclosures
  - test with the real load disconnected first

Safe relay boot state:

  relay boot pump off
  relay boot heater off
  relay boot fan off

Pause automation:

  disarm

Resume:

  arm

Emergency cleanup:

  safe next
  reboot
  cron clear
  rule clear
  timer clear

Use dry-run before testing risky workflows:

  dryrun on
  relay on pump
  relay off pump
  dryrun off
