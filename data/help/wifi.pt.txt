WI-FI
=====

Estado e procura:

  wifi status
  wifi net
  wifi scan
  ip

Guardar e ligar:

  wifi save "SSID" "password"
  wifi reconnect
  wifi wait 30

DHCP ou IP fixa:

  wifi dhcp on
  wifi static IP GATEWAY MASK DNS1 DNS2

Recuperacao:

  wifi recover
  wifi sdkreset --yes
  wifi diag

Apos upload serie, tools/upload.sh ja executa wifi sdkreset.

Erros comuns:

  201 no_ap_found   SSID nao visivel para o ESP8266
  202 auth_fail     verificar password e seguranca do AP
  beacon_timeout    sinal, interferencia ou carga do AP

O ESP8266 so usa Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz.
