FICHEIROS
=========

KernelESP usa LittleFS.

Caminhos comuns:

  /etc/config.txt
  /etc/boot.sh
  /home/
  /www/
  /help/
  /var/log/

Comandos:

  ls /
  cat /etc/motd
  head /var/log/kernel.log
  tail /var/log/kernel.log
  grep text /path/file
  find / text
  wc /path/file
  du /
  du -h /
  df
  df -h

Pipes:

  cat /var/log/kernel.log | grep wifi | tail -n 5
  dmesg | grep boot
  ls /help | wc -l
  health | tee /home/health.txt

Criar:

  write /home/test.sh echo hello
  append /home/test.sh free
  sh /home/test.sh
  rm /home/test.sh
