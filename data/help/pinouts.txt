PINOUTS
=======

This section shows the main ESP8266 board/module pinout sheets used by
KernelESP:

  ESP-12F module
  NodeMCU / Wemos D1 mini development boards
  ESP-01 module

Open the images above, or use the direct links:

  /pinout-esp12f.svg
  /pinout-esp8266-boards.svg
  /pinout-esp01.svg

Green pins are usually preferred for relays and general I/O. Yellow pins affect
boot mode and must be handled carefully. Red pins are risky for normal loads or
are used internally by flash, serial or boot.

Always compare the drawing with the exact silkscreen on your board before
wiring real hardware.
