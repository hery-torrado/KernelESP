BACKUP Y PERFILES
=================

Crear backup:

  backup

Descargar por web:

  /backup?key=<key>

Incluye ficheros importantes de /etc, /home, /pkg y /profiles.

Restaurar solo si confias en la copia:

  restore /home/backup.txt --yes

Perfiles:

  profile save known-good
  profile list
  profile show known-good
  profile load known-good --yes
  profile rm known-good

Flujo recomendado: configurar, probar, guardar perfil y descargar backup.
